SPIDER SWING

Story: A new wave of signal drones has scattered 18 spider-rings across the skyline. Swing, zip and wall-run to recover them. Master momentum — your webs are real pendulums.

Tip: Swing side-to-side to pump, release at the bottom for max speed. Zip (right click) to perch. Hold Q to shorten web for tighter arcs.

Pointer-lock on desktop gives immersive look. On mobile use touch + drag on screen to look. No build step — pure Three.js.

1) Aim crosshair at building → Left Click to fire web. You attach only if surface hit within 140m.
2) You become a pendulum — W to pump forward, A/D to strafe, Q/E reel in/out.
3) Press SPACE at lowest point to launch far. Right-Click zip instantly pulls you to aim point.
4) Collect gold rings for score. Fall resets at 20 m.
