Moonlit Parcel Dash

Night has fallen on Luna Outpost. Your tiny rover must dash across the dark dunes, snatch glowing parcels drifting in the cold, dodge tumbling moon rocks, and sprint to the delivery beacon before oxygen runs out.

How to Play

• Move with WASD / Arrows or drag on canvas / mobile pads.
• Touch parcels to collect — they pulse gold.
• Rocks drift slowly; hitting one stuns you for 0.7 s, flashes red & costs 1.5 s.
• Collect all 6 parcels, then touch the pink beacon tower to win.
• Beat the 45-second countdown. Palette bar drains as time passes.
• Press R or Play Again to replay instantly.
