A polished top-down battle-royale prototype. Drop into a 2.4 km arena, scavenge, survive the collapsing safe zone, outgun 11 hostile bots. No luck — just positioning, aim, and nerve.

How to Play

  • WASD move, Shift sprint — use cover (walls, containers, rocks)
  • Mouse aim, Click / Space shoot, R reload (30-round mag)
  • Stay inside the shrinking safe zone; outside you take escalating damage
  • Loot ammo crates and medkits — they pulse when ready
  • 11 bots hunt, strafe, and take cover. Last alive wins.

What Makes It “PUBG-like”

  • Large arena with hard cover and sightlines
  • Magazine + reserve ammo, reload window, hit feedback
  • Phased collapsing zone with visual pressure
  • Elimination feed, remaining count, minimap
  • Third-person top-down camera — responsive and fair on mouse or touch
