How to Play

  • Objective: Fly through 20 glowing rings. Perfect center = bonus.
  • Controls: WASD / Arrow Keys to move. Smooth, responsive.
  • Hazards: Dark storm clouds & red balloons = -1 life & screen shake. 3 hits = crash.
  • Boundaries: Don't scrape the ground or sky ceiling — you'll stall.
  • Feel: Engine hum, ring chimes, tilt & particles. Turn sound on!
Built lightweight — canvas + procedural sky. No external assets. Gauntlet bar: Pilotwings Resort · 60 fps target
