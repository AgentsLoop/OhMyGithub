How to Play

  • Move with Arrow Keys or WASD. Swipe on mobile.
  • Eat golden food to grow and score +10.
  • Don't hit walls or yourself. That's game over.
  • You cannot reverse directly — e.g., going Right you can't instantly go Left.
  • Speed ramps up every 50 points. Survive as long as you can.
