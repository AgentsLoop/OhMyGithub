NEXUS COMMAND

Terran Dominion vs Zerg Swarm — Mini StarCraft

BRIEFING

Commander, the Zerg have infested Sector 7. Establish your Command Center, harvest minerals and vespene gas, build Supply Depots and Barracks, and raise an army of Marines and Siege Tanks. The enemy Hive will keep producing Zerglings — strike before you're overwhelmed.

  • SCV (50 min) — Worker, builds & gathers
  • Marine (50 min) — Reliable infantry, 6 dmg
  • Tank (150 min / 50 gas) — Heavy hitter, 22 dmg, slow
  • Buildings need SCV + resources. Supply Depots raise cap by 8.

Tip: Select SCV → click Build → place building. Right-click minerals to harvest.

VICTORY

BUILD — Select SCV first

HOW TO PLAY

  • Select: Left-click / drag box. Ctrl+click adds, Shift queues/extends selection.
  • Move/Attack: Right-click ground = move, right-click enemy = attack.
  • A + Click: Attack-move (engage anything on the way).
  • S: Stop, H: Hold position. Ctrl+1..0: Assign control group. 1..0: Recall. Double-tap centers camera. Ctrl+Shift+Num: Add to group.
  • SCV: Select SCV → Build buttons → click valid (green) location.
  • Gather: Right-click blue Mineral Field or green Vespene Geyser. SCV auto-returns to Command Center.
  • Supply: Build Supply Depots or you'll be blocked.
  • Minimap: Click/drag to jump camera. Groups also clickable below minimap.
